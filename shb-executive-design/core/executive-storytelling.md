# Executive Storytelling · v2

## Objective
Turn information into a management narrative without overstating what the evidence proves.

## Default reading order
1. Executive message / conclusion
2. Primary evidence
3. Secondary drivers / decomposition
4. Implication / watchpoint / action
5. Source / definition / caveat

## Headline behavior
Prefer insight-led headlines over topic labels.

Weak: `Tình hình huy động vốn`

Better: `Huy động tăng chậm hơn tín dụng, gia tăng yêu cầu bổ sung nguồn TT1 trong các tháng cuối năm`

The headline must be directly supported by the evidence shown.

## Management language
Prefer concise, natural, institutional Vietnamese.
- State the number or change first when useful.
- Name the comparison basis.
- Use specific implications.
- Avoid filler, vague adjectives, and generic consultant language.

## Decision framing
A management page should normally help answer at least one:
- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- Compared with what?
- What matters for management?
- What should be monitored, decided, or acted on?

## Density
Target one main conclusion per page/slide. If multiple conclusions compete equally, split or create an overview + detail structure.

## Causality discipline
Do not convert correlation or sequence into causal language unless supported by evidence.
