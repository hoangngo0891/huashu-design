# Quality Review · v2

Before delivery, review the output on these dimensions:

## 1. Accuracy
- Numbers, units, dates, timing basis, sources, and definitions are correct.
- Comparisons such as YoY/YTD/vs plan are explicitly labeled.
- No fabricated values, unsupported causality, or misleading visual encodings.

## 2. Message clarity
- The main takeaway is visible within 3–5 seconds.
- The visual hierarchy matches the intended reading order.

## 3. Institutional fit
- The output feels appropriate for SHB and the intended audience.
- Brand is recognisable without overwhelming the content.

## 4. Fit for purpose
- The visual form suits the analytical question and deliverable type.
- A table is not used where a trend chart is needed, and vice versa.

## 5. Language quality
- Wording is concise, natural, and specific.
- Avoid generic AI or consultant-style filler.

## 6. Visual craft
- Alignment, spacing, typography, contrast, and density are disciplined.
- Decorative elements do not compete with evidence.

## 7. Editability and maintainability
- Preserve editable text, charts, tables, and shapes where feasible.
- Sources, labels, and assumptions are maintainable by the business team.

A visually attractive output that fails accuracy, message clarity, or fit for purpose is not acceptable.
